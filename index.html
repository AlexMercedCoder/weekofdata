Subscribe on Luma How it gets scheduled

Progress toward 100 subscribers

The first Week of Data gets scheduled when the Luma calendar reaches 100 subscribers. At that point the date and city are chosen with the subscribers and announced on the calendar.

Luma does not publish subscriber counts, so this page does not show a number. The calendar itself is the place to check for the announcement.

Goal: 100 calendar subscribers

Status: collecting subscribers. Date and location: not yet chosen.

Subscribe to the calendar

Subscribing on Luma adds the Week of Data calendar to your account, so the announcement and every listed event reach you directly.

Data events you can join now

While Week of Data waits for its first date, these community calendars run events today:

  • Data Lakehouse Hub events: talks and meetups on open lakehouse architecture.
  • Agentic Lakehouse events: sessions on AI agents working with lakehouse data.
